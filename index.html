Source-Segregation Protocol — "Two Bins, One Bag"

University Design Thinking CP5 Prototype Operating Guidelines

Effective municipal solid waste management begins at the household level by preventing organic moisture from contaminating recyclable dry materials. Examine each photographic waste specimen and sort it into the appropriate receptacle:

GREEN BIN — WET WASTE

  • Biodegradable kitchen & organic waste
  • Fruit & vegetable peels, banana skins, rotten produce
  • Leftover cooked food scraps & crushed eggshells
  • Used tea leaves, coffee grounds, wilted flowers

BLUE BIN — DRY WASTE

  • Non-biodegradable & recyclable materials
  • Crushed plastic bottles, containers & wrappers
  • Newspapers, office paper sheets & cardboard boxes
  • Aluminium beverage cans, metal tins & glass bottles
Interaction Methods: Drag the photographic waste specimen card directly onto the Green or Blue bin, or click either bin / quick-deposit button. Your accuracy, score, and completion time are logged automatically to the Admin Dashboard.
